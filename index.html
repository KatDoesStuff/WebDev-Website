Who am I?


Hiya, I'm Kat! I'm a self-taught digital artist and BCS student from NZ.
I love designing characters - especially anything that's gnarly and fantasy-based - and sometimes I open up for commissions!

I usually always have a few pre-made designs up for purchase, and I'm going to be creating prints soon as well. The art I make usually ranges between cartoon-like and semi-realistic, though I often like to combine the two, so keep an eye out if that's the kind of art you're interested in!

Next, I'm looking into making text-based games using python (with GUI elements) and I'm working to improve my skills in animation, so in the future both game-commissions and animated-commissions will be on the table.
