Digital Art


My digital art journey began in 2021 on an old computer with a failing graphics card, a computer mouse, and MS paint. I've had plenty of ups and downs when it comes to my art, but over the last 6 years I've met many new friends, partook in a lot of cool events, challenges, and competitions, and overall had a huge amount of fun.

I've come a long way in my art journey, and I'm excited to take the skills i've learned this year into 2027!

Costuming and Cosplay


Costuming work and Cosplay are two more hobbies of mine, and over the last couple years I have enetered my work into competitions (using the costume pictured here) and I've won a couple awards for my work.

I think the joy of costuming and cosplay-creation is being able to draw something, or even just have an idea in your mind, and then make it come to life in 3D, and I'm excited to try new and more difficult costume projects in the future.
