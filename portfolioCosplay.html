The Creature from the Woods


'The Creature' is a costume I made for Toi's wearable arts challenege, using the set theme 'Imaginary Friend' as a guide. When designing, I like to push ideas to the limit, expanding upon them as much as I can - 'Yes, and...'ing them, in a way. I already knew exactly what the majority of my competitors would be creating for this theme, being costumes with bright colours and cartoony smiles, and so I chose to take a rather different route instead.

I have always loved all things folklore, and when I was a kid, everything spooky was pretty cool. Skulls, bones, and mushrooms were what I enjoyed, and I wanted to make something that reflected that rather than your usual trope-y representation of an imaginary friend. Kids can be terrifying, after all, so why couldn't their 'imaginary friends' be, too?

I got to work, putting pencil to paper (and then stylus to tablet) and eventually, I came up with a reference sheet that I rather liked.
